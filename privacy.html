Privacy policy — न्यौपाने वंशावली · Neopane Family Tree

यो एप न्यौपाने परिवारको वंशावली राख्न र परिवारका सदस्यबीच जानकारी बाँड्न बनाइएको हो। यो कुनै व्यापारिक सेवा होइन।

This app keeps the Neopane family tree and shares family information among relatives. It is not a commercial service.

के जानकारी राखिन्छ · What is kept

परिवारका सदस्यको नाम, नाता, फोटो, इतिहास, उपलब्धि, जन्म/मृत्यु वर्ष, गोत्र र थर — सबैले देख्न सक्छन्। हालको ठेगाना, फोन, इमेल, अन्य अभिलेख र कागजात — लग इन गरेका सदस्यले मात्र देख्छन्।

Names, relationships, photos, history, achievements, birth/death years, gotra and thari are public. Present address, phone, email, other records and documents are shown only to logged-in members. The family admin enters and maintains this information in a Google Sheet owned by the family.

स्थान · Location

“नजिकका आफन्त” प्रयोग गर्दा मात्र फोनको स्थान लिइन्छ, त्यो खोजको लागि मात्र प्रयोग हुन्छ र कतै राखिँदैन।

Your phone’s location is used only when you tap “Near me”, only for that one search, and it is never stored.

लग इन · Login

Members log in with a phone number and a password given by the admin. The password is stored only as a scrambled hash. “Keep me logged in” saves a signed key on your phone (not the password); the admin can end it at any time.

बाँडफाँड · Sharing

The information is not sold or shared with anyone outside the family. It is stored with Google (Google Sheets and Google Drive) under the family admin’s account.

सच्याउने वा हटाउने · Corrections and removal

आफ्नो जानकारी सच्याउन वा हटाउन एडमिनलाई सम्पर्क गर्नुहोस् (सदस्यले एपबाटै “एडमिनलाई अनुरोध” पठाउन सक्नुहुन्छ)।

To correct or remove your information, contact the family admin (members can use “Apply to Admin” in the app).
